Official Arcade Point Calculation Formulas

Only Skill Badges, Arcade Games, and Trivia count toward Arcade points

Arcade Games = 1.0 Pt

Level 1, Level 2, Level 3, Base Camp, Voyage, Adventure, Simulator, and Trail badges.

Trivia Badges = 1.0 Pt

All monthly Trivia games, Spans and Plans, and weekly knowledge challenges.

Special Games = 2.0 Pts

Special Monthly Arcade Game Badges award 2 points each toward your total score.

2 Skill Badges = 1.0 Pt

Every 2 completed Google Skill Badges award 1 Arcade Point (0.5 pts per badge). Course completion badges excluded.

Finding Your Public Profile URL

  1. 1
    Log into your account on Google Skills or Google Cloud SkillBoost.
  2. 2
    Navigate to your Profile page and click "Make Profile Public".
  3. 3
    Copy the public profile URL from your address bar (e.g. https://www.skills.google/public_profiles/907d1ea5...) and paste it into the input box!
